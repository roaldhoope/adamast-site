ADAMAST

In the golden age of Portuguese discovery, sailors spoke of Adamastor —the giant of the Cape, the personification of the fierce storms and jagged rocks that stood in their way. Adamastor, from the Greek root: Adamastos. The Untameable. The Unconquerable.

This philosophy is etched into the very molecular structure of our rubber. Our outsoles are not merely "made"—they are formed under extreme heat and immense pressure. The result - a dense, grippy and resilient rubber that refuses to wear down. For the runner who refuses to give up.
